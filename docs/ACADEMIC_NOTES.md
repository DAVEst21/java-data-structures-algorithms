# Academic report mapping

The original academic reports were reviewed locally without modifying or reorganizing their source folder. This repository includes only two extracted historical captures, not the complete reports or their cover pages. Filenames below identify the original evidence; they are not links or runtime dependencies.

The reports date from 2025. The current-status column includes the subsequent corrections; it does not imply those corrections appeared in the historical reports. Their screenshots and self-evaluation tables describe a historical state and were not treated as proof that the current source works. Narrative text, selected screenshots, and current source were compared. The most detailed review covered arrays, linked structures, priority queues, calculator, recursion, housing statistics, and KNN. Other reports were inspected for purpose, structure, representative screenshots, and correspondence to the retained modules.
